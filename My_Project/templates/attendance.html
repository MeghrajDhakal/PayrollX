How Attendance Affects Pay

📊

Proportional Pay

Pay is calculated as:
Monthly CTC × (Days Present ÷ Working Days)

⚠️

LOP Deduction

LOP days are deducted additionally on top of attendance adjustment. Use LOP for unapproved absences.

💡

Example

If CTC = ₹10L/yr & 20/22 days present:
Pay = ₹83,333 × (20/22) = ₹75,757
